# QuickQR

A fast, free QR code generator that runs entirely in your browser.

### Features

* Generate QR codes for URLs, Wi-Fi, email, phone, WhatsApp, locations, and text
* Customize colors and add a logo
* Download as PNG or SVG
* Recent QR codes saved locally
* No account or backend required

Built with React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.

## Privacy

QR codes are generated locally in your browser. Your data is never uploaded or stored on a server.
